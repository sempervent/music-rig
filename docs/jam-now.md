## Recipe (BPM 96 · 4/4)

1. **Rhythm** — start a Zoned Kit groove (track 1).  
2. **Tone** — bring miniKORG (track 2) for 8 bars.  
3. **Live** — play a clean guitar/bass phrase into track 8.  
4. **Loop** — capture one RC-1 phrase (default loop order: RC-1 → KAOSS).  
5. **Wet** — send one layer through AUX / SPACE (DIRTY or SPACE board).  
6. **Transition** — KAOSS moment (feed **TASCAM OUT 3/4** → KAOSS → **IN 15/16**).  
7. **Subtract** — mute/remove two layers; leave groove + one voice.  
8. **STOP** — Ableton stop; RC-1 stop/undo; lower monitors.

## Recovery (one move)
